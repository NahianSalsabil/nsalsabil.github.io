This work, with Dr. Tanzima Hashem, defines the **Green Route Optimization** problem: find a route that minimizes air and noise pollution and maximizes urban green space, while keeping travel distance within a constraint. The approach uses reinforcement learning with a reward function that balances these environmental factors against distance. On real-world datasets, it lowers pollution exposure and adds green space with only a modest increase in distance.
