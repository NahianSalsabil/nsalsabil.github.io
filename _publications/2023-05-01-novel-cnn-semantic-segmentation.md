This work began as my undergraduate thesis at BUET, supervised by Dr. Md. Monirul Islam. It introduces two pooling methods for CNN-based semantic segmentation:

- **Wavelet pooling** preserves fine features and edges.
- **Pixel binning** reduces feature-map resolution to widen the receptive field.

Both methods are evaluated on the CamVid benchmark.
