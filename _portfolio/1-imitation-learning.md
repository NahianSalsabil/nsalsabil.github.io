An imitation learning model that learns from an expert's driving data and reproduces the expert's commands in the same or similar situations.

- Data were collected in the **CARLA** simulator to stand in for real-world driving.
- The model is a **ResNet-18** policy trained with **behavior cloning**.
- It needs only a small amount of training data.

**Tech:** Python, PyTorch, CARLA, ResNet-18
