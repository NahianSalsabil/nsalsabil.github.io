A coreference resolution dataset for Bangla in the style of LitBank, built under the supervision of Dr. Md. Eunus Ali. Chapters of popular Bangla novels, about 1,500 words each, were annotated in the Brat standoff format to link pronouns to the nouns they refer to. The dataset has 6,000 noun–pronoun pair tokens.
